Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

GymLog is a workout tracker. This page explains what information GymLog keeps, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to create your account and show who is signed in.
  • What you log: workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes, body-weight entries and any photos you choose to add.

We do not access your contacts, Gmail, Google Drive or any other Google data. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you: saving your training, showing your history, records and progress, and syncing between your devices. We never sell your data and never share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Ireland). Security rules make sure each account can read and change only its own data, and photos are kept in private storage. A copy of your data is also kept on your device so the app works without a connection at the gym.

Your choices

  • You can export your data at any time from Settings → Data & backup.
  • You can edit or delete any workout, entry or photo inside the app.
  • To delete your account and all of its data (workouts, history, records, body weight and photos), open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. It is removed right away.
  • If you cannot open the app, open an issue at github.com/ItayMizrachi/Gym/issues or use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen. We delete it within 30 days.
  • You can remove GymLog's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

GymLog's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
